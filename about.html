About

CapturedPerspectives

A modern photography studio centred on people and real moments.

CapturedPerspectives approaches photography with a balance of documentary observation and refined composition. The goal is simple: make people comfortable, notice the details and create photographs that remain meaningful.

This redesigned prototype gives the company a stronger professional identity while keeping the experience easy to understand. Every photography category has its own dedicated page, allowing visitors to explore exactly the work that is relevant to them.
